\## 3. Development Principles

1\. Make one small improvement at a time.

2\. Develop and test locally before deployment.

3\. Preserve existing research results and reproducibility.

4\. Avoid unnecessary models, datasets, and complexity.

5\. Maintain privacy, transparency, and responsible-use messaging.

6\. Commit and push only after successful local testing.

7\. Keep IELTS and scholarship applications a priority.



\## 4. Development Workflow
