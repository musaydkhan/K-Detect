\## Dataset Selection Principles



1\. Never assume public access means unrestricted reuse.

2\. Preserve the original source and license of every sample.

3\. Keep human, AI-generated, and human-edited AI text distinguishable.

4\. Record generation model and prompt metadata where applicable.

5\. Prevent source-document leakage across data splits.

6\. Never include test samples in training.

7\. Keep a record of dataset versions and download dates.
