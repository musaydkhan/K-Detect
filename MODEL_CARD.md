# K-Detect Model Card

**Project:** K-Detect  
**Version:** 0.1.0 (Development)  
**Status:** Research and prototype stage  
**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. Overview

K-Detect is an open-source project exploring linguistic analysis and AI-generated text detection.

Its objective is to develop transparent and accessible methods for examining writing patterns while investigating the limitations and potential biases of automated AI-text detection.

## 2. Intended Use

Potential applications include:

- Educational exploration of writing patterns.
- Academic research into AI-generated text detection.
- Linguistic feature analysis.
- Experimental evaluation of detection methods.
- Studying false positives and false negatives.

## 3. Current Capabilities

The initial prototype focuses on basic linguistic analysis, including:

- Word and sentence counts.
- Vocabulary diversity estimates.
- Average sentence length.
- Sentence-length variation.
- Repeated-word patterns.
- Selected formulaic expressions and transitions.

These features describe observable characteristics of text. They do not independently establish whether text was generated by AI.

## 4. Model Information

No validated AI-text classification model is currently established as part of this project.

Future model integrations will be documented here, including:

- Model name and version.
- Model architecture.
- Source and licensing information.
- Training or evaluation dataset.
- Intended use and limitations.

## 5. Evaluation Strategy

Future detection experiments should evaluate:

- Accuracy.
- Precision.
- Recall.
- F1-score.
- False positive rate.
- False negative rate.
- Performance across different writing backgrounds.

Evaluation datasets should contain appropriately licensed or consented human-written, AI-generated, and human-edited AI-generated texts.

Data should be separated by author or source where possible to reduce data leakage.

## 6. Bias and Fairness

AI-text detectors may produce different results across language backgrounds and writing styles.

K-Detect aims to investigate performance differences involving:

- Native and non-native English writers.
- Different English proficiency levels.
- Academic and non-academic writing.
- Different academic disciplines.
- Human-edited AI-generated text.

No fairness or bias claims will be made without supporting evaluation.

## 7. Limitations

K-Detect may:

- Incorrectly flag human-written text.
- Fail to identify AI-generated text.
- Be affected by editing, paraphrasing, or translation.
- Produce unreliable results on short documents.
- Perform differently across writing styles and language backgrounds.

No detection score should be treated as proof of authorship.
